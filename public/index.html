Forecast Accuracy
How accurate is the AI? This page compares what the model predicted vs what customers actually ordered over the last 12 months.
Industry context: In B2B fashion wholesale, a forecast error of 20–40% is considered normal and acceptable — because orders are unpredictable (customers don't order every month). Our best brands are at 7–11% error, which is exceptional. Brands with high errors are affected by one-off bulk clearance orders from a small number of customers — not a model failure.

Why are CAT and BENCH errors high? Both brands have a small number of customers who place huge one-off clearance orders (e.g. 34,000 units from one customer in January 2026 — 5× normal monthly demand). The model correctly predicts regular demand but can't predict these one-off bulk orders. These customers have been tagged separately so future model runs will exclude them from training data.
