Forecast Accuracy

Validation period: April 2026 holdout — model trained on Jan 2023–Mar 2026 data, tested against real April 2026 invoices the model never saw.

What is Error %?
How far the forecast was from what actually happened. 0% = perfect. Industry standard for B2B fashion is 20–40%. Below 20% is exceptional.
What is the Ratio?
Forecast ÷ Actual. A ratio of 1.0× is perfect. Above 1.0× means over-forecast, below 1.0× means under-forecast. Acceptable range: 0.8×–1.4×.
What is Walk-Forward?
Testing the model on 3 separate future periods it never trained on. Each window proves the model genuinely predicts — not just memorises past data.
